## 1) Page job

Operational LP that:

1. Captures a **lead** (problem micro-step → light form + SMS/phone consent).  
2. Offers optional **early-buy micro-SKU** after lead only.  
3. Routes close to **LAUNCH + product → DM/call / Launch Sprint apply**.  
4. Sets up later **SaaS + standing Grok bots** upsell (backend — not this page’s close).

**Page qualifies and creates desire. Human closes.**

## 6) Copy frames (LOCKED from funnel-tbd-proposals-v1.md)

- **H1:** Get your free personalized SaaS launch + bot ops roadmap — in under 30 seconds.  
- **Problem Q:** What’s your main problem with launching / running an online or SaaS business right now?  
  Options: (1) unclear offer / who it’s for (2) leads without scammy ads (3) everything falls on me — no systems/bots (4) don’t know what to build next  
- **Anti:** Not another link-in-bio VSL. Not a motivational course dump. Systems to launch and run the business — with Grok bots on standing duties.  
- **Anti-fit:** Might not be for you if you want a silver bullet instead of installing systems.  
- **Early buy:** Launch Sprint Starter · $97 · install checklist + first Grok bot duty brief + path into Launch Sprint apply/DM · after lead only  
- **Apply fields:** First name, email, phone + SMS consent, what you’re launching, stage (Idea/Building/Live/Revenue), main problem, optional link → Apply for Launch Sprint → DM/call (not checkout)  
- **SaaS tiers (later):** Launch Desk / Operator Floor / Studio Bench  
- **CTA whitelist:** Start / Get roadmap / Apply / Book setup call.  
- **Locked Sentence:** systems to build, launch, and grow SaaS and online businesses; Grok bots on standing duties.
