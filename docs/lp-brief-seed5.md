## 1) Page job

One operational landing page that:

1. Captures a **lead** (light form).
2. Offers an optional **early purchase**.
3. Routes the real close to **LAUNCH + product → DM/call** (Launch Sprint apply path).
4. Sets up later **SaaS / standing Grok bots** upsell — not on this page as the close.

**Page qualifies and creates desire. Call/DM closes.**

---

## 2) House locks (non-negotiable)

| Lock | Implication for the page |
|------|--------------------------|
| Close = LAUNCH + product → DM/call | Primary CTA after lead = book/apply/DM — **never** VSL → order form |
| Unique mix = SaaS launch + standing Grok bots | Outcome language is launch systems + bots on duty — not “organic content coach” or golf mastermind |
| Instagram-first, no AI slop, no ad-looking | Dark/clean operator aesthetic; real artifacts; no CF “Claim Your Amazing Product” chrome |
| Cole VSL→order = FAIL close | Study for craft (outcome + steps headline, problem block) only — **do not install** as our close |

---

## 3) Who it’s for

**Buyer:** people launching digital / SaaS / online businesses (first ~200 should be the buyer).  
**Not for:** $1M+ luxury-status mastermind buyers, health/pain VSL buyers, webinar-ad shoppers.

**Anti-category line (pin #4):** one sentence that ejects wrong fit — e.g. *Not another link-in-bio VSL. Not a motivational course dump. Systems to launch and run the business — with Grok bots on standing duties.*

---

## 4) Funnel path this page supports

```
IG / traffic
  → LP (this brief)
    → Lead form (problem micro-step → contact + SMS consent)
      → Optional early-buy module (after lead, never instead of human close)
        → LAUNCH + product → DM / call / Launch Sprint apply
          → Later: SaaS onboarding + standing Grok bots upsell
```

**Shape we train toward (from seed labels):**  
Nexus-style diagnostic lead + Hormozi speed/outcome H1 + Ryan/Taki apply→call close — **minus** Cole VSL→order and luxury $1M gates.

## 5) Page architecture (sections)

### A. Hero
- **H1 structure (pin #1):** Outcome + personalization + speed.  
  Draft frame: *Get your free personalized [SaaS launch / bot-ops] roadmap — in under 30 seconds.*  
  (Final outcome phrase TBD with Funnel Manager / Editor on signed blueprint.)
- One primary CTA above the fold (Start / Get roadmap / Apply).
- Optional honest volume line **or** hold proof for section C — not both stacked as hype.
- Mobile-first; dark/clean or firm-simple; **one** primary CTA.

### B. Problem-first micro-step (pin #2)
- Single question before heavy fields: *What’s your main problem with launching / running [SaaS or online business]?*
- Dropdown or 3–4 options (exact options from Funnel Manager when blueprint signs).
- CTA: **Start** / **Let’s start** — then reveal contact fields.

### C. Proof
- Prefer **one named peer card** (role + concrete result) over empty carousels.  
- Or one honest “Requested by X…” line if volume is real.  
- **No** fake logo walls, **no** empty testimonial shells (FAIL pin #5).

### D. What you get / ladder (pin #8)
- Free diagnostic / roadmap claim.
- What happens next in plain language: early buy option → Launch Sprint / call → bots on standing duties.
- Tone: **tactical, not motivational** (pin #7).

### E. Anti-category + who it’s for
- Short “for / not for” block.
- Anti-category one-liner.

### F. Form block (pin #5)
- Fields: name, email, phone, **one** qualifier (stage / main problem already captured).
- Clear phone/SMS consent (Hormozi-style clarity, house-safe wording).
- Submit routes to **DM/call / Launch Sprint apply** — not checkout.
- CTA verbs: Get roadmap / Start / Apply / Book call — **never** “Claim Your Amazing Product.”

### G. Optional early-purchase module
- Appears **after** lead success (or as secondary module below fold).
- Small LAUNCH / product SKU — never replaces human close.
- Specs TBD with Funnel Manager on signed blueprint (price, SKU, fulfillment).

### H. Footer / trust
- Operator-real screenshots / launch artifacts — not stock hustle.
- Clean `<title>` and meta — no leftover template names (Cole FAIL).
