# Train Pins — Seed Batch 5 (LP Builder pack)
**House lock:** Close = LAUNCH + product → DM/call. Pins below are house-safe craft only.  
**Path we train:** lead form → early buy option → LAUNCH+product → DM/call → later SaaS upsell.  
**Source:** `funnel-audit-seed5.md` (Hormozi, Ryan Pineda, Taki Moore, Devin Jatho/Nexus, Dr Cole Handy Jones).

---

## Top 8 train pins (steal)

1. **Outcome + personalization + speed H1** — e.g. structure of “Get Your Free Personalized [OUTCOME]… in under 30 seconds” (Hormozi / Nexus). Swap outcome to SaaS launch / Grok-bot result.
2. **Problem-first micro-step** — one question (“What’s your main problem with…?”) before contact fields (Nexus Atlas). Raises intent; feels helpful, not spammy.
3. **Honest volume or one named proof card** — “Requested by X…” *or* one named peer with role/followers (Nexus Troy card). No empty testimonial shells.
4. **Anti-category filter line** — “Not another [bad category]…” (Ryan). One line that ejects wrong-fit traffic.
5. **Light form → phone/SMS consent → human close** — name/email/phone + one qualifier; consent copy clear (Hormozi/Ryan). Route to DM/call, not checkout.
6. **Audition / apply language** — “We don’t sell, we audition” / application-only (Taki / Ryan). Protects call quality; status flip.
7. **Tactical not motivational** — workshop/FAQ tone: zero hype talks, concrete next steps (Hormozi). Matches no-AI-slop brand.
8. **Offer ladder on page, close off-page** — free diagnostic → early buy/LAUNCH → DM/call → SaaS bots upsell. Page qualifies; call closes.

---

## Top 5 FAIL-for-us (do not copy)

1. **VSL → order form as default close** (Dr Cole CF) — page-as-close violates house lock.
2. **“Claim Your Amazing Product” / ClickFunnels chrome** — ad-looking; template titles in `<title>` destroy trust.
3. **Luxury $1M + status-gate aesthetic as our gate** (Ryan golf mastermind) — wrong audience for launchers.
4. **FB webinar CPA hero / ad economics as front door** (Taki gift page pattern) — not IG-native, ad-looking.
5. **Empty proof carousels / fake logo walls** — if testimonials don’t load real quotes, don’t ship the block.
